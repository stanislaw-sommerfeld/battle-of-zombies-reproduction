# World

## The Unwriting

The world's laws were written in the Ledger, kept in the Scriptorium by the Scribes. Fifty years ago it burned. Wherever a page came to rest, its law still holds in that area as an **Edict**. Settlements cling to helpful Edicts, creatures adapt to harmful ones, and between them lies the **Blank**.

The Ledger's ink survived. It crawls, pools and *writes*: whatever it covers slowly becomes still. Scholars called this the **Rubric**. The **Cinder Choir** worship it as the Ledger healing itself.
